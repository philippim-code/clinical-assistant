PTA Severity Calculator

Enter PTA values to see severity.

PTA guide: No Loss (<25 dB), Mild (26–40 dB), Moderate (41–55 dB), Moderate to Severe (56–69 dB), Severe to Profound (70–95 dB), Unaidable (>95 dB).

How to Calculate PTA

Add the hearing thresholds at 500 Hz, 1000 Hz, and 2000 Hz for one ear, then divide the total by 3.

(500 Hz + 1000 Hz + 2000 Hz) ÷ 3 = PTA

Calculate each ear separately. Use the measured thresholds from the audiogram.

Referral Considerations

Common findings that may warrant medical evaluation can include sudden or rapidly changing hearing, unilateral or asymmetric findings, ear pain, drainage, visible abnormalities, excessive or impacted cerumen, dizziness, tinnitus concerns, or an air-bone gap. Follow applicable scope-of-practice requirements, company policy, and referral standards.

A portable, browser-based workflow assistant built to make clinical documentation faster, clearer, and more consistent.

🩺

Purpose

Clinical Assistant supports the day-to-day workflow of a Hearing Instrument Specialist. It organizes commonly completed appointment steps and turns selected items into a concise Sycle-ready note.

It is a documentation aid—not a substitute for clinical judgment, company policy, required testing, or medical referral standards.

✨

Core Features

  • HAE, Aftercare, Delivery, and Annual Retest workflows
  • Generated notes with automatic copy
  • Unfinished appointment autosave and resume
  • Saved Outcomes with pending/completed tracking
  • Clinical tools, quick references, and customizable presets
🔒

Data & Privacy

Local storage only

Clinical Assistant does not require an account or send appointment information to an online database. Drafts, settings, and Saved Outcomes are stored in the browser's local storage on the computer being used.

Important: Use minimal patient identifiers. Browser data can be cleared, and saved information does not automatically move to another computer. Export Saved Outcomes before changing computers or clearing browser data.
